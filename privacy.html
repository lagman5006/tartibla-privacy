O‘zbekcha

Tartibla — uyg‘onish va uxlash vaqtini belgilash, kunni rejalashtirish va natijalarni kuzatish uchun iOS ilovasi. Bu sahifa ilova qaysi ma’lumotlarni yig‘ishi, ularni qanday ishlatishi va siz ularni qanday o‘chirishingiz mumkinligini tushuntiradi.

Telefoningizda qoladigan ma’lumotlar

Kun tartibi, vazifalar, natijalar statistikasi, budilniklar, fokus taymeri tarixi va ilova limiti sozlamalari faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi va serverga yuborilmaydi. Harakat sensori faqat o‘tirib-turish missiyasida takrorlarni sanash uchun ishlatiladi, ma’lumot qurilmadan chiqmaydi. Kamera va mikrofon ishlatilmaydi.

Serverga yuboriladigan ma’lumotlar

Apple yoki Google orqali kirganingizda quyidagilar himoyalangan serverimizda (Supabase) saqlanadi:

  • email manzil va ism (Apple yoki Google bergan bo‘lsa);
  • kirish usuli (Apple yoki Google);
  • tanishuv savollaridagi javoblar: uyg‘onish va uxlash vaqti, maqsadlar, budilnik missiyasi turi;
  • promo kod (agar kiritsangiz);
  • platforma, ilova versiyasi, ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan va oxirgi marta ochilgan vaqt.

Bu ma’lumotlar hisobingizni yuritish, ilovani yaxshilash uchun umumiy statistika va hamkor blogerlar promo kodlari hisobini yuritish uchun ishlatiladi.

Hisobsiz davom etish rejimida serverga hech qanday ma’lumot yuborilmaydi — hammasi faqat telefoningizda qoladi.

English

Tartibla is an iOS app for setting wake-up and bedtime, planning your day hour by hour and tracking your progress. This page explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

Data that stays on your device

Your schedule, tasks, progress statistics, alarms, focus timer history and app limit settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our server. The motion sensor is used only to count squats during the squat mission; this data never leaves the device. The camera and microphone are not used.

Data sent to our server

When you sign in with Apple or Google, the following is stored on our secured server (Supabase):

  • email address and name (if provided by Apple or Google);
  • sign-in method (Apple or Google);
  • your onboarding answers: wake-up and bedtime, goals, alarm mission type;
  • promo code (if you enter one);
  • platform, app version, sign-up date and last opened time.

This data is used to operate your account, to produce aggregate statistics that help us improve the app, and to count sign-ups for partner promo codes.

If you choose Continue without an account, nothing is sent to our server — all data stays on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites and do not use the advertising identifier.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Third-party services

  • Apple and Google for sign-in, under their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase as the server that stores the account data listed above.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, tap Profile → Delete account. Your account and all of your server data are deleted immediately. On-device data is removed when you delete the app. You can also request deletion by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
